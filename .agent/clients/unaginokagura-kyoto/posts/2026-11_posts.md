Our unaju is prepared with our original cooking method for a soft and fluffy texture. Choose from half-eel, three-quarter-eel, or whole-eel portions depending on your time and appetite.

Takeout is also available by advance reservation, perfect for enjoying on the train.

Table reservation requests are available through the booking link in our profile. Same-day reservation requests are accepted depending on availability.
