Traveling solo in Kyoto and looking for a place to eat on your own?

Unagi no Kagura Kyoto is a small 16-seat restaurant on B1F of Kyoto Avanti, directly connected to Kyoto Station. Counter seats are available, so it is an easy place to stop for a meal by yourself.

Each unaju, grilled eel over rice, comes with soup, pickles, and a small dessert, so a single order makes a complete meal. It is prepared with our original cooking method for a soft and fluffy texture, and extra tare sauce is served on the side so you can adjust the flavor to your preference.

The restaurant is fully non-smoking and convenient for lunch before sightseeing, shopping, or catching your train.

Table reservation requests are available through the booking link in our profile. Same-day reservation requests are accepted depending on availability.
